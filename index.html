Before work begins

Learn what the system is before you rely on it.

An older house altered over the years may hide cable from several eras, run in different ways, in the same wall cavities. Past owners may have left no drawings, permits or notes. Any home's records can have gaps like these.

Before the purchase closes or in the first weeks after possession, collect the seller disclosures, inspection report and any ESA paperwork you can find. List what is unknown, because the written scope should say what was checked and what was not.

Is an electrical review a substitute for the house inspection I already have?

No. A home inspection looks at the whole house, while an electrical review by a Licensed Electrical Contractor focuses on the wiring, panel and equipment. Treat the two as complements, and share the inspection report with the contractor.

What can I do if the seller has no records of past wiring changes?

Ask the seller in writing what was changed and who did it, and look for ESA paperwork or invoices. Where nothing exists, ask the contractor to state which findings rest on visible equipment and which are assumptions.

Why do two proposals differ when nobody knows what is behind the walls?

Differences often come from assumptions about the existing wiring rather than the work itself. A sound quote lists what was seen, what was not reachable and what changes if concealed conditions turn out different.

Should the assessment happen before or after closing on a house?

Either timing can work. Before closing, findings can still shape the purchase conditions, while after closing you can plan changes with full access. Check whether the purchase terms allow a visit and who must permit entry.

Who signs the contract once I send the enquiry form?

A provider or Licensed Electrical Contractor signs any contract; this site only informs. First your enquiry is read and reviewed; nothing here is scheduled or priced, and no electrical work is done. If you contact one, it confirms its licence and identity separately; check that licence through ESA before agreeing to anything, from job and cost to timing.
